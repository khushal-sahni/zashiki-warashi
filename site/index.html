What it keeps

A machine-local catalog of your projects, one-button start/stop via your login shell, Compose/DB peek, and deep-links into Cursor, Finder, and Compass. Glue for tools you already have — not a Docker GUI, not Portainer, not a folder bookmark.

How it works

  1. Add a folder or scan roots.
  2. Select a project → Start / Stop / Restart.
  3. Peek the Stack, copy a URI, open Compass for mongo.

What you need

macOS. Docker Desktop or OrbStack for Compose stacks. Builds are unsigned for now — first open may need right-click → Open, or xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine on the app.

座敷童子

Named after the house spirit that looks after the place. Observe existing repos. Keep overrides in app data. Do not require per-repo YAML.
